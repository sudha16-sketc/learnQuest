# Product Vision

## Tagline
"LearnQuest — Turn Skills Into Achievements, Achievements Into Opportunities."

## Problem Statement
Online learning platforms often measure progress through quiz scores, completion certificates, and platform-specific achievements. These signals do not always demonstrate practical competence, and learners can struggle to turn their learning into credible opportunities.

## Solution
LearnQuest combines gamified practical learning with verifiable achievements on MST Blockchain. Users demonstrate real skills through assessments, earn verifiable achievements, and access sponsored skill challenges and opportunities.

## Target Users
- Students and self-taught developers
- Web3 and blockchain developers
- Educators and learning communities
- Companies seeking skilled participants
- Sponsors funding skill-based challenges and competitions

## Core User Journey
1. **Play and learn:** Explore a 3D game world and complete AI-generated quests
2. **Demonstrate mastery:** Solve progressively harder assessments and practical challenges
3. **Earn achievements:** Receive achievements after meeting defined mastery criteria
4. **Verify achievements:** Selected achievements can be recorded/verified through MST Blockchain
5. **Unlock opportunities:** Participate in sponsored competitions, premium challenges, and skill-based programs

## MVP Scope
- Preserve existing 3D game, AI, backend, auth, XP/streaks/leaderboards
- Add mastery-based achievement system (distinguish game progress vs verifiable mastery)
- Add sponsored challenges (admin-controlled for MVP)
- Integrate MST Blockchain for verifiable achievement records and challenge-related transactions
- Integrate official MST Vibe Kit and SDK where applicable
